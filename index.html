      Keybindings

      Click a binding, then press the key you want. Escape always cancels and can't be rebound. The map-move keys pan while held.

      Map builder

      Pick a tool, then drag on the map. Right-drag erases. Q / wheel rotates low cover. Painting the same unit/door/switch/chest/pickup onto its tile removes it. The link tool: click a switch, then a door, to link or unlink them.
