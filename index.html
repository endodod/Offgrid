LoadoutDrag gear from the locker onto a slot

Locker

Drag a piece onto a slot, or tap it and then tap the slot. Drag anything back here to take it off. Gear is shared: a piece on one squadmate is not available to another.

SettingsKeybindings

Click a binding, then press the key you want. Escape always cancels and can't be rebound.

Map builder

Pick a tool, then drag on the map. Right-drag erases. Q / wheel rotates low cover. Painting the same unit/door/switch/chest/pickup onto its tile removes it. The link tool: click a switch, then a door, to link or unlink them.
